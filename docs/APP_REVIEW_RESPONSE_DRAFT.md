## 1. What the app does and the primary problem it solves

eSign : Sign Any Docs is a local-first document completion workspace for people who receive paper forms or existing PDFs and need to finish them from an iPhone or iPad. The complete workflow is performed in one place: scan a paper document with automatic edge detection, import an existing PDF or image, add a transparent handwritten signature, text, dates, checkboxes, or a custom transparent stamp, save the work as a draft on the device, and export or share the completed PDF when ready.

The primary problem is the gap between receiving a paper form and returning a correctly completed digital document. The app is designed for quick, private completion of a document without requiring an account or uploading the source document to our server.

## 2. Intended users

The intended users are individuals, freelancers, tenants, students, small-business operators, and office staff who occasionally receive forms or agreements and need to complete them on a phone or tablet. It is not intended to be a team-based enterprise signature platform or a document-management replacement.

## 3. Why the app is useful in addition to existing apps

The product is focused on a short, local-first scan-to-complete workflow rather than a recipient/envelope workflow. Its differentiating features are:

- automatic document-edge detection with manual capture and page adjustment;
- editable local drafts that remain available before subscription or export;
- reusable transparent signatures and uploaded transparent stamp artwork;
- direct manipulation of every field with move, resize, and rotate controls;
- purpose-built Agreement, Invoice, and Consent document kits that place editable starter fields for common workflows;
- no account requirement and no server-side document storage in the first release.

These features are implemented in the submitted Flutter application and are not only marketing screenshots.

## 4. Beta testing

Before resubmission we tested the complete flow on a physical iPhone 17 Pro Max and tested the Android counterpart during development. Testing covered scanning, import, multi-page documents, signature drawing, text/date/checkbox/stamp placement, draft restoration, export, share, and purchase/restore flows. Owner QA feedback led to improvements to scanner edge detection, transparent signature and stamp rendering, annotation drag/resize/rotate behavior, draft persistence, PDF annotation alignment, and the in-app completed-document viewer.
