eSign : Sign Any Docs

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

eSign : Sign Any Docs is designed to process documents on your device.

Information handled by the app

  • Documents, scans, signatures, annotations, stamps, and drafts are stored locally unless you explicitly export or share them.
  • Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document.
  • Photo-library and file access are used only when you choose to import a document or image.
  • Subscription purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store payment-card details.
  • We do not sell personal information or use documents for advertising or profiling.

Sharing and export

The app does not upload document content automatically. When you choose Export or Share, the operating system delivers the file to the destination you select.

Retention and deletion

Local drafts remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete drafts and exported copies using the app or your device's file controls.

Purchases

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Trial eligibility, billing, refunds, and cancellation are controlled by the applicable store.
