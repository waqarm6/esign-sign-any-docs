# Privacy Policy — eSign : Sign Any Docs

**Effective date: October 4, 2026**

eSign : Sign Any Docs is designed to process documents on your device.

## Information handled by the app

- Documents, scans, signatures, annotations, stamps, and drafts you create are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.
- Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document.
- Photo-library and file access are used only when you choose to import a document or image.
- Subscription purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.
- The app does not sell personal information or use documents for advertising or profiling.

## Sharing and export

The app does not upload your document content automatically. When you choose Export or Share, the operating system's share sheet or file provider delivers the file to the destination you select.

## Retention and deletion

Local drafts remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete drafts and exported copies using the app or your device's file controls.

## Purchases

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Trial eligibility, billing, refunds, and cancellation are controlled by the applicable store.

## Contact

For support, please open an issue at [github.com/waqarm6/esign-sign-any-docs/issues](https://github.com/waqarm6/esign-sign-any-docs/issues).

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with any revision.
